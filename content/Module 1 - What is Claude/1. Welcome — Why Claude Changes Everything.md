Most people are using AI like a search engine.

They type a question. Get an answer. Close the tab.

That's not what we're here for.

By the end of this course, Claude won't be a tool you "use." It'll be a teammate that builds apps, automates your workflows, manages your projects, and ships real work while you focus on the stuff that actually matters.

This isn't theory. This is hands-on, build-as-you-learn, walk-away-with-a-real-AI-workflow type of training.

Here's what's ahead:

- **Module 1–2:** Understand Claude + get Claude Code installed and running
- **Module 3:** Turn Claude into your personal thinking partner for strategy, writing, and research
- **Module 4–5:** Set up project memory and connect Claude to your entire tool stack
- **Module 6–8:** Build custom commands, automate workflows, and run multi-agent AI teams
- **Module 9:** Vibe code a full app from scratch, no coding experience needed
- **Module 10:** Design your personal AI workspace that runs your business

You don't need to be technical. You don't need to know how to code. You just need to follow along and do the work.

Let's get dangerous.
