You've probably used ChatGPT. Maybe Gemini. So let's address the obvious question:

*"Why should I learn Claude when I already have ChatGPT?"*

Fair question. Here's the real answer.

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Great at general conversation. Massive plugin ecosystem. The default AI for most people.

But it tends to over-explain, give generic answers, and gets sloppy on complex reasoning tasks. It's the "good enough for most people" option.

### Gemini (Google)

Strong at research. Integrated with Google Workspace. Good multimodal capabilities.

But inconsistent quality. Sometimes brilliant, sometimes confidently wrong. Google ships fast but breaks things.

### Claude (Anthropic)

Best-in-class reasoning. Longest context window, 200K tokens, meaning it can hold an entire codebase in its head. Most honest model on the market, trained to say *"I don't know"* instead of making things up.

And the killer feature? **Claude Code.**

Claude is the ONLY AI that can operate your terminal. Read your files. Write code. Run commands. Build entire applications. Connect to external tools. Run multiple AI agents in parallel.

ChatGPT can't do this. Gemini can't do this.

This is why we're here.

### The Bottom Line

ChatGPT is for chatting. Gemini is for Googling. **Claude is for building.**
