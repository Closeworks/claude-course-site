Time to get your hands dirty. Let's have a real conversation with Claude, not a "hello world," but something actually useful.

Go to **claude.ai** and try these:

**For strategy:**

*"I'm thinking about starting a SaaS that helps [your industry] with [problem]. Analyze this idea, strengths, weaknesses, and what I should build first."*

**For writing:**

*"Write me a cold outreach email. I do [what you do] for [who you serve]. Keep it under 150 words. Make it sound human, not salesy."*

**For research:**

*"Compare [Option A], [Option B], and [Option C] for [your use case]. Give me a recommendation with reasoning in a table."*

**For brainstorming:**

*"Give me 10 content ideas for someone who teaches [your topic] to [your audience]. Focus on hooks that grab attention."*

### The 5 Rules of Great Prompting

1. **Be specific** about what you want
2. **Give context**, who you are, what you're building
3. **Assign a role**, "Act as a senior marketer..."
4. **Specify the format**, bullet points, table, code, etc.
5. **Iterate**, Claude gets better the more context you give it

These aren't optional. They're the difference between "meh" and "holy crap."
