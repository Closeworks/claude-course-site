Understanding how Claude works under the hood will instantly make you better at using it.

You don't need to be technical. Just understand three concepts.

### Concept 1: The Context Window

Think of this as Claude's short-term memory.

Everything you type, and everything Claude responds with, fills up this memory. Claude can hold about **200,000 tokens** at once. That's roughly 150,000 words. An entire novel. An entire codebase.

This means you can paste massive documents, long instructions, and complex multi-part requests. Claude processes ALL of it.

Most people underuse this. They ask one-line questions. That's like hiring a consultant and only giving them 30 seconds of context.

**Give Claude more. Get more back.**

### Concept 2: Constitutional AI

Claude isn't just trained on human feedback. It has a set of principles, like a constitution, that guide its behavior.

What this means for you:
- Claude is more honest than other models
- It pushes back on bad ideas (this is a feature, not a bug)
- It says "I'm not sure" instead of making things up
- It's designed to be helpful without being harmful

### Concept 3: Extended Thinking

Claude has a "deep work" mode. Instead of firing off the first thing that comes to mind, it pauses to reason step by step.

You trigger it naturally:
- *"Think carefully about this..."*
- *"Consider all the tradeoffs..."*
- *"Step by step, work through..."*

The difference between a quick answer and a thought-through answer is massive, especially for strategy, code architecture, and complex decisions.
