Here's a problem you'll hit fast:

Every time you start a new Claude Code session, Claude has **amnesia**. It doesn't remember your last conversation. Doesn't know your tech stack. Doesn't know your preferences.

CLAUDE.md fixes this permanently.

**CLAUDE.md is a markdown file that Claude reads automatically at the start of every session.** It contains everything Claude needs to know about your project, preferences, and rules.

### The Difference

**Without CLAUDE.md:**
You: *"Add a new page to my dashboard"*
Claude: *"What framework? What structure? TypeScript?"*

**With CLAUDE.md:**
You: *"Add a new page to my dashboard"*
Claude: *immediately creates the right file, right folder, right conventions*

It's the difference between briefing a new freelancer every day vs. working with someone who already knows everything.
