Claude Code has three layers of memory.

### Global (~/.claude/CLAUDE.md)
Applies to ALL projects. Your personal preferences.

### Project (./CLAUDE.md)
This project only. Shared with team via Git.

### User (.claude/settings.json)
Private to you. Not in Git.

When there's a conflict, **Project wins** (most specific).

## Action Step

1. Create your global CLAUDE.md at `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`.
2. Mark this lesson as **DONE**.
