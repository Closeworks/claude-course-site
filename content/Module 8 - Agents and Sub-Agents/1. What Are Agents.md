One Claude is powerful. Multiple Claudes in parallel? That's a **team**.

Agents are separate Claude instances launched from your session. Each has its own focus, works independently, and reports back when done.

**Single Claude:** Research, then Frontend, then Backend, then Tests (sequential, slow)
**Agents:** Research + Frontend + Backend + Tests (parallel, fast)

You're the CEO. Claude is your team.
