### Offer Creation

*"I help businesses implement AI. Target: agencies at $500K-$2M/year. 2-week sprints, $3K-$5K each. Structure an offer: 'We help [X] achieve [Y] without [Z].' Give me 3 angles."*

### Revenue Modeling

*"Model revenue: 3 tiers ($27/$47/$97), distribution 50/35/15, 8% monthly churn, starting 20 customers growing 15% MoM. Show 12 months."*

### Market Analysis

*"Analyze the AI automation agency market. Saturation? Underserved niches? Pricing? Where's it heading? What do you recommend for someone entering?"*

Claude synthesizes a personalized analysis that would take a human consultant hours.
