There's a version of Claude that most people never access.

When you ask Claude a simple question, it gives you a fast answer. Fine for easy stuff.

But for complex problems, strategy, architecture, debugging, analysis, you want Claude to actually *think* before it speaks.

That's extended thinking.

### How to Trigger It

You don't need a special command. Just frame your request to demand depth:

*"Think carefully about this before responding..."*

*"Consider all the tradeoffs and give me your best recommendation..."*

*"Step by step, work through this problem..."*

### The Difference

**Without extended thinking:**
*"What tech stack should I use?"*
*"Next.js with PostgreSQL."* (surface-level, generic)

**With extended thinking:**
*"I'm a solo founder building a B2B SaaS for courier companies. Needs real-time tracking, charts, role-based access. I know React. I have 2 weeks to launch. Think through the tradeoffs and give me a reasoned recommendation."*

Claude considers your timeline, experience, use case, and gives a detailed, justified answer.

Night and day.

## Action Step

1. Take a complex decision you're currently facing. Prompt Claude with full context and ask it to think deeply.
2. Mark this lesson as **DONE**.
