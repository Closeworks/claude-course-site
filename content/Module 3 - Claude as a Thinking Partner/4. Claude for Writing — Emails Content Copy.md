Claude is an exceptional writer, when you give it direction.

### Emails

*"Follow-up email to a prospect who attended my webinar but didn't book. My offer: AI automation, $2,500. Friendly but confident, not desperate. Under 150 words. Soft CTA."*

### Social Media

*"5 Instagram carousel concepts about [topic]. For each: hook (slide 1), 5-7 slide titles, CTA (last slide). Educational but not boring."*

### Landing Pages

*"Above-the-fold copy. Product: [what]. Target: [who]. Headline, subheadline, 3 bullets, CTA button text. Clean, no hype."*

### The Editing Workflow

1. **Generate** a first draft
2. **Critique**, *"What's weak about this?"*
3. **Rewrite**, *"Fix those weaknesses"*
4. **Polish**, *"Make the opening punchier"*

Three rounds gets you 90% of the way to perfect.
