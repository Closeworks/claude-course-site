Claude is the best research assistant you've ever had, if you know how to use it.

### Analyzing Documents

In Claude Code, point Claude at any file:

*"Read @proposal.pdf and summarize the key points. Flag anything risky or unclear."*

*"Analyze @data.csv and tell me the top 3 trends."*

### Competitive Research

*"I'm entering the [industry] space targeting [audience]. Research this market: competitors, pricing, services, gaps. Format as a table."*

### Decision Frameworks

*"I'm deciding between [Option A] and [Option B]. Create a comparison matrix: cost, features, setup, scalability, recommendation."*

### Summarizing

*"Here's a 5,000-word article. Give me: 3-sentence summary, 5 key takeaways, and what I should do differently."*

Most people Google, read 10 articles, then synthesize. With Claude, you get the synthesis in seconds.
