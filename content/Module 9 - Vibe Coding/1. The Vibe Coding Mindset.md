Old way: Learn language, learn framework, write code, debug, ship eventually.

New way: **Describe what you want. Claude builds it.**

### The 5 Principles

1. **Describe the WHAT, not the HOW**
2. **Iterate in small steps**, feature by feature
3. **Review everything**, trust but verify
4. **Commit after every win**, safety net
5. **Start over when stuck**, faster than fixing bad foundations

You're not the developer. You're the product manager.
