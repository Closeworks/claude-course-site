*"I want to build [idea]. Before we code, interview me to understand what I need. One question at a time."*

After the interview:

*"Create a project plan: tech stack, feature list (MVP vs nice-to-have), file structure, build order."*

**Review the plan.** It's 100x easier to fix a plan than to fix code.

### Good MVP Rules

- 1-3 core features, not 20
- Skip auth if you don't need it yet
- Tailwind defaults look great
- Ship fast, polish later
